Who can use Lifeline Care Supply? +

The planned platform is for individual investors. Business investor accounts are not part of the current scope.

How do fixed investment packages work? +

Each package will have its own published amount, duration, contractual return structure and risk disclosures. Review all terms before deciding whether to invest.

Are investment returns guaranteed? +

No guarantee is made on this website. Any return information must be supported by the final approved product documentation, and investing involves risk.

Can I track my transactions? +

The planned investor portal will provide personal records for confirmed deposits, investments, withdrawals and other account activity.
